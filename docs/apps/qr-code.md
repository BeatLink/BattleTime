# QR code

Keeps up to eight QR codes that the NeoTime companion app made, such as links, contacts or Wi-Fi
networks, so another phone can scan them from the watch.

## Using it

- In the companion app, type the text or link, or fill in a Wi-Fi network's name and password, and
  give it a caption, or keep the one suggested. The phone makes the code and adds it to the end of
  the watch's list.
- The companion app also lists the codes on the watch, to rename them, move them up or down, or
  remove one or all of them.
- Open the app to see the codes by caption, and tap one to show it. The code is black on white
  with its caption underneath, the screen goes to full brightness and stays on while the app is
  open. Only the code tapped is read from the watch's storage.
- The code is drawn as large as fits; codes too large to scan on the screen are refused by the
  companion app.
- Without codes the app says to make them in the companion app.
- Codes added while the app is open show the next time it opens or the screen turns on.
