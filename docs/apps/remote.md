# Remote

Up to nine buttons that the NeoTime companion app sets up. Tapping one tells the phone which button
it was, and the phone runs the action chosen for it, such as playing music or taking a photo.

## Using it

- In the companion app, choose the buttons, an icon for each and what each one does. The phone
  writes them to `/remote/buttons` on the watch.
- Tap a **button** to send it to the phone. The watch gives a short buzz.
- One button fills the screen, two sit side by side, three or four make a 2 by 2 grid and five to
  nine use three columns.
- The app reads the buttons again each time it opens or the screen turns on.
- Without buttons the app says to choose them in the companion app.
