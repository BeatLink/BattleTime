# Navigation

Turn-by-turn directions from the phone, ported from InfiniTime's Navigation. It shows the next
turn's arrow, the instruction, the distance to the turn and how much of the route is done.

## Using it

- The phone must be connected and its navigation app must send directions through the companion
  app. When the app opens it asks the phone for the current step.
- The arrow shows the next turn and the line under it says what to do there.
- The distance is in metres and kilometres, or in feet and miles if the watch is set to imperial
  units in Settings.
- The bar shows how much of the route is done. It turns red past 90 percent.
- Until the phone sends a step, the app shows **Navigation** and **--M**.
- There are no buttons; the side button goes back.
